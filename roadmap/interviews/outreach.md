# Outreach messages

Short, honest and without a pitch. The only ask is a 20 to 30 minute conversation about how the person's team handles tech debt. Do not name the
product, link the landing page or say you are building something in these messages (the script shows the page at the very end).

Placeholders: `[Name]`, `[Your name]`, `[link]` (a scheduling link or two proposed times). Every message is under about 120 words and has one
follow-up nudge, sent **5 to 7 days** later. If there is no reply to the nudge, stop. Do not send a third message.

⚠️ Rules of thumb: send more messages than you need (plan for roughly one reply in ten from cold contacts, and more from warm ones; these are not
sourced benchmarks). Say it is research, say how long it takes, and say what happens with the answers. Never imply an interview leads to a sale.

## 1. LinkedIn DM (personal network first)

> Hi [Name], I am researching how engineering teams deal with tech debt, in particular the TODOs and workarounds that get left in the code. You lead
> a team at [company type], so I would value your view. Could we talk for 20 to 25 minutes this or next week? It is research only: I am not selling
> anything, and I will share what I learn from the interviews if you want it. [link]

**Nudge (5 to 7 days later):**

> Hi [Name], a quick nudge on my note above. If it is not a good time, no problem at all. Even a 15 minute chat or a "pass" helps me. Thanks, [Your name]

## 2. Email (cold or warm)

**Subject:** 20 minutes on how your team handles tech debt?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I am [Your name], a developer who maintains an open-source tool for tracking TODO comments. I am researching how engineering managers and tech leads
> keep track of tech debt, and what goes wrong when nobody does. I would like to hear how it works on your team: a 20 to 25 minute call, with
> questions about what actually happened, not opinions.
>
> This is research, not a sales call. Answers are anonymised, and I will send you a short summary of what I find. If you are open to it, here is my
> calendar: [link].
>
> Thanks, [Your name]

**Nudge (5 to 7 days later):**

> Hi [Name], following up on my note from last week about a short chat on tech debt. If now is a bad time, tell me and I will not write again.
> Thanks, [Your name]

## 3. Waitlist follow-up

For people who joined the waitlist. They gave their email for early access, so say so and make the interview optional.

**Subject:** You joined the waitlist: can I ask you a few questions?

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for joining the waitlist for [Brand]. You are getting this one message because you signed up. Before building anything, I am talking to
> engineering managers and tech leads about how their teams handle tech debt today. Would you give me 20 to 25 minutes? I mostly want to hear about
> what happens now, and what you tried. [link]
>
> If you would rather not, ignore this, or reply "unsubscribe" and I will remove you from the list. Thanks, [Your name]

**Nudge (5 to 7 days later):**

> Hi [Name], one reminder about the 20 minute chat on tech debt. A one-line reply with the best day works. If not, no problem, and I will not ask
> again. Thanks, [Your name]

⚠️ The landing page form has no "may we contact you for an interview" checkbox (see the README for what to do). Send this message once, to people who
answered the form, and honour any request to stop at once. Check the rules that apply to you (for example the GDPR for EU contacts and India's DPDP
Act); this is not legal advice.

## 4. Launch commenter reply

For someone who commented on a launch post (Show HN, Reddit, LinkedIn, a newsletter). Reply in the same thread first, publicly, then continue in a
private message if they agree.

**Public reply:**

> Thanks for the comment, that is a useful point. I am talking to a few people about how their teams handle tech debt. Would you be up for a short
> chat? I will send you a DM.

**Private message:**

> Hi [Name], thanks for your comment on [post]. I am researching how teams track tech debt and what happens when a TODO gets forgotten. Could we
> talk for 20 to 25 minutes? No pitch: I want to hear your experience, and I will share the findings. [link]

**Nudge (5 to 7 days later):**

> Hi [Name], a gentle nudge in case my message got buried. If you are busy, no worries. Thanks, [Your name]

## 5. Community post (r/developersIndia, a Python or DevOps meetup channel)

Read the community's rules first and ask a moderator if self-posts for research are allowed. Post once, as yourself, and do not link a product.

**Title:** Engineering managers and tech leads: 20 min chat about how your team handles tech debt?

> I am researching how teams track and deal with tech debt (forgotten TODOs, workarounds that outlive their reason). I am looking for engineering
> managers, tech leads and staff engineers at companies with roughly 10 to 200 developers, working in GitHub. I will ask about the last time it
> caused you a problem, what you tried and what happened. It is research only and nothing is sold. 20 to 25 minutes on a call; I will share what I
> learn in this thread. Comment or DM me if you are interested.

**Nudge (one comment, 5 to 7 days later):**

> Still looking for a few more people (EMs, tech leads, staff engineers). DM me if you have 20 minutes this week. Thanks to everyone who got in touch.

For a **meetup**, post the same text in the group's channel or ask the organiser to mention it; offer to talk in person after the event.
